# REINT CHALLENGE

This is a solution for the Gridbricks ReInt challenge by Disha Pandurangi. 

Project started 10:13 AM IST, Oct 26 2026.

## Problem Statement

A UK-based energy utility client provides electricity to its customers via renewable energy sources such as wind generators and covers any additional energy needs through buying it via the day-ahead market. 

The electricity generated by renewable wind energy is dependent on weather conditions. To estimate the quantity of electricity they would need to buy from the day-ahead market, they rely on weather predictions and make their decision on the amount on the previous day.

To aid their decision-making, I am implementing a web-based product that:
- helps the client understand and analyse the latest renewable generation forecasts available on the [FUELHH dataset](https://bmrs.elexon.co.uk/api-documentation/endpoint/datasets/FUELHH/stream) 
- evaluate the accuracy of the renewable generation forecast quantitatively

so that the team can make decision on buying energy from the day-ahead market, while considering quantified error.

This tool is not meant to prompt the amount of energy that the team would need to buy on a particular day **directly**, but instead helps them make informed decisions based on generation forecast data analysis.

## Product Description

The tech stack used is Python, React and Docker.
